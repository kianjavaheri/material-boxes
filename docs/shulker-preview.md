# Shulker box preview

See what's inside a shulker box without placing it.

**Right-click** a shulker box in any inventory or container screen, with nothing held on your cursor. A panel opens over the screen showing its 27 slots and its name. Hover an item for its normal tooltip.

- **View only.** Nothing can be moved in or out, and the shulker box is never picked up or changed. It works on any server, because it only reads the contents the server already sends with the item.
- **Closing:** Esc, E, right-click, or a click outside the panel. This closes only the preview, not the inventory or chest you're in.
- **Blocked while open:** other clicks, scrolling and hotbar keys, so nothing happens to the slot underneath by accident.
- **Unchanged:** left-click still picks up the shulker box, and shift-right-click still quick-moves it.
- **Auto-close:** the preview closes by itself if the shulker box is moved out of its slot.

Material Boxes can't open a shulker box in your inventory to take items out or put them in. The server only lets you do that with placed shulker boxes.
